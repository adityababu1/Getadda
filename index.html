1. Finality of Wallet Deposits

Get Adda utilizes a closed-loop digital wallet system. Any funds deposited by the user into the 'Deposit Balance' are intended exclusively for participating in cash tournaments on the platform. Once a successful deposit is made via our payment gateways, the transaction is deemed final. Users cannot withdraw the 'Deposit Balance' directly back to their bank accounts; only 'Winnings Balance' earned through successful tournament gameplay is eligible for withdrawal.

2. Non-Refundable Tournament Entry Fees

When a user voluntarily registers for a tournament and the requisite entry fee is deducted from their wallet, the fee becomes strictly non-refundable. Users cannot claim a refund if they forget to join the match room, experience personal internet connectivity issues, or fail to follow the tournament instructions. It is the user's sole responsibility to be present and prepared at the scheduled match time.

3. Platform-Initiated Cancellations (100% Refunds)

In the rare event that a tournament is officially cancelled or voided by the Get Adda Administration—due to server crashes, technical glitches on our end, or failure to meet the minimum required participant threshold—a full 100% refund of the deducted entry fee will be automatically reversed into the respective users' Get Adda wallets. These automated refunds are typically processed instantly, but may take up to 24 hours in exceptional cases.

4. Payment Gateway Failures and Reversals

If a user attempts to add funds and the amount is debited from their bank account or UPI application but fails to reflect in the Get Adda wallet due to banking network latency, the transaction is marked as 'Failed'. In such scenarios, the payment gateway automatically initiates a reverse transfer. This refund process is controlled by the banking institution and typically takes 3 to 7 standard business days to credit back to the source account.

5. Chargebacks and Fraudulent Activity

Any attempt to file a fraudulent chargeback with your bank or credit card provider after utilizing the deposited funds on the Get Adda platform will result in an immediate, permanent ban. We maintain detailed transaction and gameplay logs and will contest fraudulent chargebacks vigorously with our payment partners.
